1. Overview

Global Music Jam ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by diku1968, is a collaborative music production and audio recording application designed to connect musicians worldwide. This Privacy Policy informs users about our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information, microphone access, and third-party advertising services (Google AdMob) when using our App.

By downloading, installing, or using Global Music Jam, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Microphone & Audio Recording (RECORD_AUDIO)

Core Feature Audio Disclosure: Global Music Jam requires explicit access to your device microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO). This permission is used strictly and exclusively to allow musicians to record vocal and instrument tracks into their multitrack projects and transmit live peer-to-peer audio during active jam sessions.

  • No Background Recording: We do not record, listen, or buffer ambient audio when the app is minimized, in the background, or when you have not tapped the Record or Live Jam button.
  • Explicit Control: Audio recording only begins upon user interaction (such as pressing the Record button in the Studio). You may pause, stop, or delete your recorded audio stems at any time.
  • No Audio Selling: We never sell, rent, or analyze your private voice or instrumental recordings for third-party marketing or advertising purposes.

3. Advertising Policy (Google AdMob)

AdMob Integration: Global Music Jam uses Google AdMob to display advertising (Banner Ads and Interstitial Ads) to support free access to our audio engine and cloud features.

Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers and device data to deliver ads, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID): Used to measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and serve non-personalized or personalized ads based on your preferences.
  • Device Information: Device type, manufacturer, operating system version, and general non-precise location (country/city level derived from IP).
  • Ad Interaction Metrics: Impressions, click events, and ad view duration.

4. Information We Collect and Third-Party Services

To provide synchronization and multitrack storage, the App integrates standard Google services:

  • Authentication (Firebase Auth): When you sign in (e.g. via Google Sign-In or email), we receive your display name, email address, and profile photo URL to create your musician identity.
  • Cloud Firestore & Cloud Storage: Stores project metadata, track arrangements, and stems you choose to publish or save.
  • Network Status (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE & INTERNET): Used to check real-time connection status for audio streaming, cloud saving, and ad loading.
  • Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH & BLUETOOTH_CONNECT): Used to detect and route low-latency wireless headphones and MIDI instruments during recording sessions.

5. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

We retain your account profile and uploaded tracks for as long as your account remains active. You maintain full ownership and control over your creative work:

  • You can delete individual recorded tracks, instrument layers, or projects directly from the Studio at any time.
  • You can request complete deletion of your account and all associated cloud data by contacting us at the email provided below. All personal data and audio files will be permanently purged within 30 days.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Global Music Jam is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately remove such data from our servers.

7. Security

We value your trust in providing your information and musical recordings. All communications with our servers utilize industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption. Access to Firestore rules is strictly configured to ensure only project collaborators can access private audio stems.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically for any changes.
