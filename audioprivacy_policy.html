Privacy Policy

Application: Audio-to-MIDI & Chords Finder • Package: com.diku1968.audiomidi • Last Updated: October 19, 2026

🛡️ Zero Cloud Audio Transmission Guarantee

Audio-to-MIDI & Chords Finder executes all pitch tracking, YIN autocorrelation algorithms, and chroma chord recognition 100% locally in volatile RAM on your physical Android hardware. No audio recordings, microphone captures, or voice samples are ever saved to disk or transmitted across the internet.

1. Overview

Audio-to-MIDI & Chords Finder ("we", "our", or "the Application") respects your privacy. This policy explains what permissions our app requires, why they are needed, and how advertising networks handle anonymous device telemetry in accordance with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement, GDPR, and CCPA.

2. Microphone Permission (RECORD_AUDIO)

The Application requests the Android RECORD_AUDIO permission strictly to provide real-time digital audio analysis:

  • Ephemeral Processing: Audio samples from your microphone are analyzed immediately in 11.6ms buffer windows and discarded.
  • No Audio Storage: The App never writes or archives audio recordings to internal or external storage.
  • No Cloud Servers: No voice audio, songs, or instrument frequencies are uploaded to any server.

3. File & Media Access (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO)

Storage permissions are utilized solely when initiated by the user:

  • To select an audio file from your device to analyze notes and chords.
  • To save generated Standard MIDI files (.mid) to your device's Downloads or Music directory.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Application displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob to support continuous maintenance and feature updates:

  • Google AdMob may collect pseudonymous device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), IP address, and coarse device metrics for fraud prevention and ad attribution.
  • You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Review Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

The Application is intended for general audiences, musicians, and audio engineers. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.
