1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Senior Sahayak ("we", "our", or "us"). We are deeply committed to respecting and protecting the privacy, dignity, and independence of our users, especially senior citizens and elderly individuals. This Privacy Policy outlines what information the app processes, how permissions are utilized, and how we protect your confidentiality.

2. Offline & Local-First Architecture

Senior Sahayak is built with a privacy-first, local-first architecture:

  • Offline Document Storage: In the "મારા દસ્તાવેજો" (My Documents) feature, your saved documents, identity cards, and notes are stored strictly locally on your device's internal storage. We do not have access to your documents, and they are never uploaded to any remote server or cloud.
  • Locally Saved Contacts & Reminders: Any emergency family contacts, doctor numbers, or medicine reminders you save remain solely on your device.

3. Android Device Permissions & How They Are Used

To provide accessible, step-by-step assistance for senior citizens, Senior Sahayak requests the following permissions strictly for in-app functionality:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) & Speech Recognition: Used exclusively when you tap the microphone button in "મને પૂછો" (Ask Assistant) to enable voice search in Gujarati, Hindi, or English. Audio is converted to text transiently. Voice audio recordings are not stored, kept, or shared.
  • Phone Call (CALL_PHONE / Intent): Used solely to initiate emergency telephone calls (such as national emergency 112, ambulance 108, or your pre-configured family contacts) after displaying an explicit confirmation dialog. The app never makes background calls or dials unauthorized numbers.
  • Storage & Photos / Camera (Optional): Used only when you choose to take a photo or upload an image to save a local document in My Documents or when asking for visual assistance in the Screenshot Guide. These files remain 100% on your device.
  • Internet Access: Required to load verified government web portals via your browser, check for app updates, and display Google AdMob advertisements.

5. Official Government Portals & Third-Party Links

Our app provides curated, direct links to official public web portals (such as myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in, incometax.gov.in, digilocker.gov.in, voters.eci.gov.in, jeevanpramaan.gov.in, beneficiary.nha.gov.in, parivahan.gov.in, passportindia.gov.in, digitalgujarat.gov.in, ugvcl.com, pgportal.gov.in, and cybercrime.gov.in).

When you tap on these links, you leave Senior Sahayak and enter external websites operated by government departments. These third-party sites have their own separate privacy policies and terms of service. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of any external site they visit.

6. Children's Privacy

Senior Sahayak is specifically designed for senior citizens and general adult family caregivers. The app does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

7. Security of Your Information

We value your trust in using Senior Sahayak. Because user data is kept locally on your physical device, we employ standard operating system sandboxing and Android security best practices. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we advise users never to share confidential PINs or OTPs with anyone.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly to this web page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We advise you to review this page periodically for any updates.
